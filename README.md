# Catastrophe Risk Simulation

## Objective

Develop a catastrophe risk model for a hypothetical homeowners insurance company.

The model will use SQL, Python, and Excel to analyze catastrophe exposure, simulate future losses using Monte Carlo methods, and calculate actuarial risk measures including Expected Loss, Value-at-Risk (VaR), and Tail Value-at-Risk (TVaR).

## Business Problem

A homeowners insurance company wants to estimate its annual financial exposure to hurricanes.

Using historical catastrophe data and a simulated portfolio of insured homes, this project estimates annual aggregate losses through Monte Carlo simulation. The results will help evaluate risk exposure and estimate the amount of capital needed to withstand extreme loss years.
